# Thoughtful compliments

Specific compliments feel more sincere than generic praise. Notice choices, energy, humor, curiosity, kindness, or a detail the person chose to share. Keep compliments proportional to how well you know someone.

Examples for inspiration: “You make even the little stories entertaining.” “I like how curious you are about things.” “That playlist has excellent main-character energy.” Do not repeatedly compliment someone after they change the subject or show discomfort.
