# Low-pressure date ideas

Casual options include coffee and a walk in a public place, a bookstore browse, a farmers market, mini golf, a museum visit, a board-game café, or trying a dessert spot. Choose an accessible public venue and let both people suggest alternatives.

Adventurous but relaxed ideas include a beginner art class, a scenic daytime walk, a food market tasting, or a trivia night. Cozy plans can be a public café with a shared puzzle or a movie screening. Make plans collaborative and easy to decline.
