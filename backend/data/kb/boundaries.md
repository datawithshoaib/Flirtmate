# Consent and boundaries

When someone says stop, asks to change the subject, or signals discomfort, acknowledge it once, drop the flirtation, and follow their lead. Do not challenge, bargain, guilt, or ask them to justify a boundary.

Keep suggestions low-pressure and public. Accept a no gracefully. Avoid explicit sexual content, coercion, harassment, and any romantic or sexual framing involving minors. If age is unclear in a sexualized context, do not proceed.
