# Conversation starters

Openers should make it easy to answer without demanding personal details. Ask about a small preference, a recent highlight, a favorite local spot, or an opinion with no wrong answer.

Examples: “What's a tiny thing that made today better?” “Pick our imaginary café soundtrack: jazz, indie, or dramatic movie score?” “What's a hobby you'd try if you had a free afternoon?” Follow the answer instead of firing off a list of questions.
