# Playful banter

Good teasing is affectionate, specific, and easy to laugh off. Joke about a harmless choice or a shared moment, never someone's body, identity, insecurity, or vulnerability. Pair a tease with warmth, and stop if it does not land.

Use callbacks to something the other person actually said. Keep a running joke light and give them room to steer. A good comeback adds a new playful angle instead of trying to win the exchange.
